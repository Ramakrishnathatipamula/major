DISEASE NATURE & MANAGEMENT STRATEGY:
Late blight of potato, caused by the oomycete Phytophthora infestans, is an aggressive foliar and tuber disease. It spreads rapidly under cool, wet weather (10°C to 22°C) with relative humidity >= 80-90% and prolonged dew or fog.
ICAR-CPRI and TNAU emphasize that disease management must be preventive: prophylactic applications before disease onset or canopy closure are far more effective than curative sprays once systemic epidemics take hold.

PROPHYLACTIC / PROTECTANT FUNGICIDE OPTIONS (CIBRC REGISTERED):
1. Mancozeb 75% WP:
   - Category: Contact protectant fungicide (FRAC Group M03).
   - Target: Prophylactic cover spray prior to symptom appearance or upon Indo-Blightcast disease warning.
   - Verified Application Rate: 0.2% concentration (2.0 to 2.5 kg/ha in 1,000 litres of water, or 2 to 2.5 g per litre of water).
   - Timing & Schedule: Apply before canopy closure or immediately when weather becomes favorable (cool, cloudy, humid). Repeat at 7 to 10 day intervals if weather remains conductive.
2. Chlorothalonil 75% WP:
   - Category: Broad-spectrum contact protectant (FRAC Group M05).
   - Target: Preventive protection on healthy foliage.
   - Verified Application Rate: 0.2% concentration (approx. 2 kg/ha in 1,000 litres of water).
   - Timing: Alternate with Mancozeb to maintain multi-site contact protection.
3. Copper Oxychloride 50% WP / Copper Hydroxide:
   - Category: Inorganic copper contact protectant (FRAC Group M01).
   - Verified Application Rate: 0.25% to 0.3% (2.5 g to 3.0 g per litre of water).

SYSTEMIC / TRANSLAMINAR CURATIVE COMBINATIONS (CIBRC REGISTERED):
Apply immediately upon noticing initial water-soaked foliar lesions or under high local disease pressure:
1. Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP:
   - Category: Translaminar systemic + contact combination.
   - Verified Application Rate: 0.3% concentration (approx. 3.0 kg/ha in 1,000 litres of water).
   - Timing: Apply at onset of first symptoms.
2. Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP (or Metalaxyl-M / Ridomil MZ):
   - Category: Phenylamide systemic + contact combination (FRAC Group 4 + M03).
   - Verified Application Rate: 0.2% to 0.25% (2.0 to 2.5 g per litre of water).
   - Resistance Rule: Do not apply Metalaxyl consecutively more than 1 to 2 times per season to prevent resistant pathogen strains from dominating.
3. Dimethomorph 50% WP (combined with Mancozeb):
   - Category: Carboxylic acid amide systemic fungicide (FRAC Group 40).
   - Verified Application Rate: 1.0 kg Dimethomorph + 2.0 kg Mancozeb per hectare.

NON-CHEMICAL CONTROL & CULTURAL PRACTICES:
1. Certified Seed: Use disease-free certified potato seed tubers.
2. Sanitation: Eliminate and destroy cull piles, volunteer potato plants, and solanaceous weeds near fields.
3. Dehaulming (Vine Killing): Cut or desiccate potato haulms/vines 10 to 14 days prior to harvest to prevent sporangia from washing into soil and infecting tubers.
4. Water Management: Avoid overhead sprinkler irrigation late in the evening.

SAFETY, PPE & ENVIRONMENTAL PRECAUTIONS:
1. Wear protective personal equipment (PPE) including gloves, face mask, eye goggles, and protective clothing during chemical handling and spraying.
2. Do not spray against the wind direction.
3. Strictly observe product label pre-harvest interval (PHI) and restricted entry interval (REI) as specified on registered manufacturer product packaging.
4. Avoid spraying near water bodies, honeybee foraging areas, or livestock grazing pastures.
5. Statutory Notice: Farmers must consult local Agricultural Extension Officers, Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK), or State Agricultural University advisories before purchasing and applying any chemical formulations.
