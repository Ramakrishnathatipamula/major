# MAIZE / CORN DISEASES: COMMON RUST, TURCICUM BLIGHT, AND GRAY LEAF SPOT

TARGET CROP: Maize / Corn (Zea mays)
GOVERNING BODIES: Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC), ICAR-Indian Institute of Maize Research (IIMR), State Agricultural Universities (TNAU, ANGRAU, PAU).

## 1. MAIZE COMMON RUST (Puccinia sorghi)

### Symptoms & Environmental Drivers
- Small, powdery, circular-to-elongated cinnamon-brown to golden-brown pustules on both upper and lower leaf surfaces.
- Favoured by moderate temperatures (16°C to 25°C) and high relative humidity (>85%) with prolonged dew periods.
- Pustules rupture epidermal tissues, accelerating transpiration and early senescence.

### CIBRC & ICAR Verified Chemical Treatments
1. Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC:
   - Dosage: 1.0 ml per litre of water (200 ml per acre in 200 L water).
   - Highly curative and preventive for cereal rusts.
2. Mancozeb 75% WP:
   - Dosage: 2.5 g per litre of water (500 g per acre).
   - Apply at the first sign of rust pustules; repeat after 10-12 days if disease conditions remain favourable.
3. Propiconazole 25% EC:
   - Dosage: 1.0 ml per litre of water (200 ml per acre).
   - Fast systemic action arresting mycelium development.

### Biological & Cultural Management
- Plant rust-resistant commercial hybrids (HQPM-1, DHM-117, Pusa HM-4, Bio-9681).
- Spray Trichoderma harzianum @ 10 g/L or Pseudomonas fluorescens @ 10 g/L.
- Destroy volunteer corn plants in field borders.

## 2. NORTHERN CORN LEAF BLIGHT / TURCICUM LEAF BLIGHT (Exserohilum turcicum)

### Symptoms
- Long, elliptical, grayish-green to tan lesions (cigar-shaped, 3 to 15 cm long) beginning on lower leaves and progressing upwards.
- Severe blighting causes foliage to appear burned or frost-damaged.

### Recommended Fungicides
1. Mancozeb 75% WP or Zineb 75% WP:
   - Dosage: 2.5 to 3.0 g per litre of water (600 g per acre in 200-250 L water).
2. Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC:
   - Dosage: 1.0 ml per litre of water.
3. Carbendazim 12% + Mancozeb 63% WP:
   - Dosage: 2.0 g per litre of water.

---
## 3. GRAY LEAF SPOT (Cercospora zeae-maydis)

### Symptoms
- Rectangular, tan-to-gray lesions strictly delimited by parallel leaf veins.
- Develops under warm (25°C to 30°C), humid (>90% RH) conditions with overcast skies.

### Treatment
- Spray Pyraclostrobin 20% WG @ 1.0 g/L or Azoxystrobin 23% SC @ 1.0 ml/L.
- Alternate with contact Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L to manage resistance.
